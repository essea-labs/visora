## Description (max 4000 chars)

```
Innerproof is a daily practice for becoming who you want to be.

Goals fade and willpower fails. But when you can clearly picture the version of yourself you're becoming, your daily decisions naturally point there. Innerproof gives you 90 seconds in the morning, then small wins all day, as proof you're getting there.

— THREE STEPS, REPEATED DAILY —

MANIFEST · One-time setup
Build a vision board for one area of your life. Pick an area, name it, write a few sentences about who you're becoming, and choose 3–5 photos that represent it.

VISUALIZE · 90 seconds, every morning
A voice-guided practice walks you through breathing, visualization, and intention-setting. Your identity statement appears on screen. You set today's one thing.

DOCUMENT · Throughout the day
Note small actions that show you're becoming who you said. A text. A no. A small choice. Tagged to your vision, kept as proof.

— WHY IT WORKS —

Athletes use mental imagery before performance for a reason: your brain rehearses the version of you who already does the thing. Tiny daily proof builds identity faster than big infrequent milestones. When the action matches who you are, you don't need to push through. You just do it.

— WHAT'S INCLUDED —

· 90-second voice-guided morning ritual, every day
· Up to 5 vision areas with personalized identity statements
· Capture small wins, tagged to your vision
· Optional witness — one person who sees the wins you choose to share
· Your archive is always yours, always exportable

— HOW IT'S DIFFERENT —

Innerproof isn't a habit tracker. There are no streaks to maintain, no scores to game, no daily counter to break. The whole point is showing up — quietly — and watching the proof stack up.

— PRICING —

7 days free. Then $24.99/year (saves 30%) or $2.99/month. Cancel anytime in Settings.

No ads. No data sold. No noise.

innerproof.app
```
