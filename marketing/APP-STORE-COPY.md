## Apple App Privacy questionnaire: data types

When you fill in App Store Connect → App Privacy:

### Photos
- **Collected?** Yes (when user picks photos for vision board / wins)
- **Linked to identity?** No
- **Used for tracking?** No
- **Purpose:** App Functionality

### Crash Data (Sentry)
- **Collected?** Yes
- **Linked to identity?** No
- **Used for tracking?** No
- **Purpose:** App Functionality (diagnostics)

### User Content (text: visions, wins, intentions)
- **Collected?** Yes (stored on device, optionally synced once auth lands)
- **Linked to identity?** Yes (once auth, via Apple user ID)
- **Used for tracking?** No
- **Purpose:** App Functionality

### Purchase History (RevenueCat receipt)
- **Collected?** Yes (subscription status only)
- **Linked to identity?** No (anonymous RevenueCat ID)
- **Used for tracking?** No
- **Purpose:** App Functionality

### Apple ID (when signed in)
- **Collected?** Yes (opaque user ID, not email)
- **Linked to identity?** Yes
- **Used for tracking?** No
- **Purpose:** App Functionality (sync)

### NOT collected
Location, contacts, browsing history, search history, identifiers for tracking, advertising data, financial info, sensitive info, audio data.

## App Review notes (paste into App Store Connect → App Review)

```
Visora is a personal development app with a daily voice guided ritual,
a vision board, and a win capture journal. The 7 day free trial unlocks
all features; after the trial users continue with a monthly or yearly
subscription managed entirely via the App Store.

To test the app:
1. After launch, walk through the onboarding (Welcome → Why → Pick areas
   → Build your vision board → Paywall). To bypass the paywall during
   review, use the sandbox tester account provided in the Sandbox tab.
2. The paywall offers a 7 day free trial; in sandbox mode this resolves
   instantly without charge.
3. Once past the paywall, the main tabs are Home, Vision, and Wins.
4. The daily ritual is accessed from Home → "Begin ritual". It's a
   ~2 minute voice guided visualisation. Audio plays through the silent
   switch.
5. Account management: tap the avatar on Home → Settings → "Manage
   subscription" deep links to the iOS Subscriptions page.

Test account (if needed for sync): username + password in the Sandbox tab.

No special hardware required. Tested on iPhone 14 Pro through 16 Pro Max
on iOS 17.x and 18.x.

Contact for review questions: review@tryvisora.com
```
