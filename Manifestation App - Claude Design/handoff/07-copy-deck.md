# Copy Deck

All product copy, in one place. Brand voice rules first; full strings second.

---

## Voice rules

1. **She, not "your future self."** Use the pronoun. It's intimate.
2. **Italicize the becoming.** Wherever the user is referred to as becoming, lean into italic emphasis — *her*, *the work*, *quietly*.
3. **Soft verbs only.** Build, picture, prove, capture, witness, settle, reach, become. Never: achieve, hustle, crush, level up, optimize, hack. *"Anchor" is a noun in the product, not a verb on a button* — see Capture / Wins copy.
4. **Lowercase eyebrows-in-uppercase, sentence-case body.** Eyebrows are CAPS for visual rhythm; nothing else is.
5. **One thought per line.** Never stack two ideas in a sentence; break with a period.
6. **Never use the word "user."** Always second-person ("you") or third-person ("she").
7. **Never apologize for the product.** "We" voice is direct and gentle.
8. **No exclamation marks. Ever.**
9. **Numbers spelled out under ten** ("ninety seconds", "one witness"), digits beyond.
10. **Em-dashes are welcome.** Anchor breathes through punctuation.

## Forbidden words

`achievement` · `goal` · `streak` · `score` · `metric` · `crush` · `hustle` · `optimize` · `hack` · `level up` · `winner` · `journey` (yes — too overused) · `unlock` (except where literal, e.g. tier benefits) · `unleash` · `revolutionary` · `game-changer`

## Morning ritual voice script (template)

Read by the chosen voice (default: Sage, soft and low).

### R01 Settle (15s)
> Welcome back. Take a breath. You don't have to do anything yet — just sit. *(pause 6s)* Soft eyes. Soft jaw.

### R02 Breathe (20s, breath-led)
> *(no language; breath cues only — the voice models 4-in, 4-out, twice)*

### R03 Picture (35s, identity)
> Now picture her. *(pause 2s)* {IDENTITY_STATEMENT, slowly} *(pause 4s)* Again. *(pause 2s)* {IDENTITY_STATEMENT, slower}

### R04 Anchor (15s, intentions)
> Today, *one* thing carries her forward: {TODAY_I_WILL}. Just that. *(pause 3s)* You don't have to do it perfectly. You just have to do it.

### R05 Send-off (5s)
> She's already moving. Go.

## Capture / Wins copy

- Empty composer placeholder: *"What's a small piece of her, today?"*
- Tag prompt: *"Which vision is this?"*
- Send button: **"Save"** *(v2 — was "Keep this" in the deck and "Anchor it" in the mocks; both rejected. The button is now neutral so "anchor" stays reserved for the method name, not a verb. No icon on the button — just the label.)*
- Top-of-sheet eyebrow: **none** *(v2 — the "Drop the anchor" eyebrow was removed. The headline `What's a small win from today?` already orients the user.)*
- Witness toggle: *"Let her see this"* (off) / *"She'll see this"* (on)
- After send, soft toast: *"Saved. ✦"* (single sparkle, terra)
- Edit window expired: *"This one's set. As it should be."*
- Soft delete: *"Released."* (with 5s undo: *"Bring it back"*)

## Witness copy

- Empty pair invite: *"Invite one person."* / *"Just one. They'll see your wins. You'll see theirs."*
- Pending: *"She's been invited. She'll arrive when she's ready."*
- Active, no wins this week: *"She's quiet today."*
- Acknowledgement (soft tap): no text, just a 800ms amber pulse on the win
- End pair confirmation: *"This ends the witnessing. Her wins won't reach you anymore. She'll know."*
