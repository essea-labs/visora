Daily wisdom — five visual treatments.

Five distinct presentations for the daily quote. Each preserves the existing amber "Daily wisdom" section heading so the rest of Home stays untouched — only the quote treatment changes. Drawing on print pull-quote tradition, postcard / postage stamp design, and minimalist editorial layouts.

V1 · Postcard + wax seal
Aged paper artefact

Cream paper card with a thin terra rule along the top edge and a tiny circular wax-seal "stamp" in the corner showing today's day number. Feels like something arrived in your mail. Inspired by handwritten postcards / vintage correspondence.
