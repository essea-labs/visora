Affirmations feed — concepts

Curated affirmation library. Surfaces on the Home tab (renamed from "Today") as a small entry point, expands into a full-screen feed where users can browse, save, and build a collection. Affirmations are tagged by life-area category so they can be personalised to the user's active visions.

Feel: Quiet entry. A horizontal strip of 3 affirmation cards sits below the ritual, headlined "Affirmations · See all →". First card is featured (slightly bigger, amber outline) — today's pick. The rest rotate based on the user's active visions.

Feel: Tap an affirmation card → full-screen takeover. One affirmation per screen, big italic typography, photo backdrop. Swipe up for next, down for previous. Save (heart, fills amber), link to a vision (tag it), or share as image. Story-like progress dots show position in the session.

Trade-off: TikTok pattern works but takes the user OUT of the home flow. Best when they want to consume — meditation mode, evening wind-down. Long-press to pause auto-advance.

Feel: Pinterest-style mood board. Reached from "See all →" on the home strip. Category chips at top (color-coded to match life areas), "Saved" tab for the user's collection. Variable-aspect cards make it feel curated, not gridded.

Trade-off: this is a fully separate screen. Lives at /affirmations. Doesn't add a new tab — entry stays through the Home strip.

All three pieces ship together as one feature. A is the daily touch (home tab strip). B is the deep mode (full-screen swipe). C is the library (explore + saved collection). Together they cover: glance · consume · curate.

Initial content: ~150-200 affirmations, curated across 6 life-area categories. Each tagged with photo + category + tone (gentle / bold / playful). Selection algorithm on Home: prioritises user's active vision categories, rotates daily.
