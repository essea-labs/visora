Where the name explanation lives

Two placements for the locked one-liner: "Visora — vision and aura. The vision you walk toward, the aura you walk in."

1 · In-app — bottom of the name screen

Onboarding step where the user enters their first name. The app's name explanation sits as a small italic line at the very bottom — quiet, discoverable, not in the way. Nice rhyme: user types their name, app explains its own.

First, your name
What should we call you?
Just so Visora can greet you. Lives on this device only.
Your first name

Visora — vision & aura
The vision you walk toward,
the aura you walk in.
Placement: bottom-anchored, ~24pt above the safe-area bottom. Two-line break for rhythm. Cream at 55% opacity so it doesn't compete with the input above.

Why here: the user is naming themselves. The app names itself back. The two acts mirror, and the explanation lands when the user is in the most receptive frame of the whole flow.

2 · Website — "The name" block, between hero and "Why Visora"

Stand-alone block on tryvisora.com. Sits as a small, contemplative pause between the marketing hero and the three "Why Visora" pillars below. Reads as an aside the curious visitor catches.
