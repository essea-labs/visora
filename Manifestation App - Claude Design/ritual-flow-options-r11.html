Daily ritual — flow rethink

The current ritual has both a visual "story" (5 timed beats with copy) AND an audio player on top — they fight each other. Three competitor-inspired directions below. Pick which feels right for the manifestation experience.

What competitors do
  • Calm — full-bleed photo backdrop, single big play button center, scrubber at the bottom. Almost no text during the experience. Audio is the entire ritual.
  • Headspace — animated breath visual center-screen, minimal text ("Breathe in… out"), play/pause + skip controls at the bottom.
  • Insight Timer — photo + title at top, timer + scrubber at bottom, no per-section text changes.
  • Waking Up — black background, breathing dot, controls auto-hide. Pure minimal listen.
  • Balance — voiceover walks you through everything. UI just shows progress + pause.

Pattern: audio is the experience. Visuals are atmosphere, not content. Controls live at the bottom.
