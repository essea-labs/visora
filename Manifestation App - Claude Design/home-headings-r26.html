Heading iterations — R26.

Four distinct treatments for the Affirmations + Wisdom sections, all on a no-scroll Home tab. Goal is something that feels considered without competing with the ritual card above. Same content in every preview — only the section heading treatment changes.

A · Tiny eyebrow

Minimal uppercase tag

Pulled back to a tiny letter-spaced uppercase tag — barely there. Lets the content (cards + quote body) carry all the weight. Closest to a "no chrome" feel without removing the heading entirely.

B · Numbered editorial

Italic number + rule

A small italic-serif number (i, ii) sits next to a hairline rule and a small uppercase label. Feels like a magazine section break. Adds a sense of progression — first the affirmations, then wisdom.

C · Conversational italic

Subline, em-dash lead-in

A short italic phrase preceded by an em-dash, like a hand-written marginal note. Conversational, low-key. Says "to embody", "to reflect on" — verbs of intent, not nouns of section.

D · No headings

Just the content + a dot
