FAQ

Honest answers.

Is this another meditation app?

No. The ritual is voice guided, but it's a visualisation of a specific version of you, not general mindfulness. And it continues all day through capturing wins.

I don't know what I want to become.

That's the most common place to begin, and Visora is built for it. You don't need the final answer. Start with one area that pulls at you: Career, Health, Love, Finance, Joy, or name your own. Add a photo that gives you the feeling, write one line as if it's already true, then let it sharpen as you practice. Clarity comes from doing it, not before.

What if I miss a day?

You miss a day. No punishment, no shame. The practice is yours to keep, skip a week if life happens.

What does the subscription include?

Everything: the full ritual, unlimited vision tiles, unlimited wins with photos, linked vision attribution. One subscription, no tiers.

What does it cost?

$2.99 / month or $29.00 / year (≈$2.42 / month, save 19%). 7 day free trial. Cancel any time in iOS Settings → Subscriptions.

What's a "win" exactly?

Anything that's evidence the becoming is real: a meeting handled, a workout finished, a boundary held. The bar is low on purpose. Small evidence stacks.

iOS only at launch. Android is on the roadmap, no firm date yet. We're getting the iOS practice right first.
